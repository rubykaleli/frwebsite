This Privacy Policy explains how Fragry collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our social perfume discovery app.

App: Fragry
Operator: ARISTOKRATI INC.
Contact: info@fragry.com

1. Introduction

Fragry is a social perfume discovery app operated by ARISTOKRATI INC. (“we,” “our,” or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use Fragry on iOS, Android, or any related website or service.

By using Fragry, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We limit collection to information needed to operate Fragry, provide social features, improve app reliability, respond to support requests, and keep the community safe.

Account Information

If you create or use an account, we may collect information such as:

  • Username
  • Display name
  • Email address, if used for login, account management, or support
  • Profile photo, if you choose to upload one
  • Account settings and preferences
  • Sign-in identifiers provided by Apple, Google, or another authentication provider, if applicable

User Content

Fragry allows users to interact with perfume-related content and other users. We may collect and store content you provide, including:

  • Comments
  • Profile information
  • Photos of perfumes that you upload
  • Likes, saves, reactions, or similar app interactions, if available
  • Follow and follower relationships
  • Reports, moderation requests, or support messages submitted by users

Photos and Media

If you upload photos of your perfumes, we collect and store those images so they can be shown in Fragry. Fragry does not access your photo library unless you choose to select or upload a photo through your device permissions.

Device, App, and Diagnostic Information

We may collect technical information to help keep Fragry reliable and secure, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Language or region settings
  • Crash logs
  • Error reports
  • Performance diagnostics
  • General technical information needed to diagnose app issues

Crash Reporting Through Sentry

We use Sentry or similar tools to help identify crashes, bugs, and performance issues. These tools may process technical information related to crashes and errors, such as device information, app version, operating system, timestamps, error details, and diagnostic logs.

We use this information only to monitor app stability, diagnose technical issues, and improve Fragry.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts
  • Display user profiles, perfume photos, comments, and other content
  • Allow users to follow each other
  • Allow users to comment on perfume posts
  • Operate, maintain, and improve Fragry
  • Detect, troubleshoot, and fix crashes or technical errors
  • Respond to support requests
  • Review reports of inappropriate content, abuse, or violations of our rules
  • Protect the safety, security, and integrity of Fragry
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Public Content and Social Features

Fragry includes social and user-generated-content features. Information you choose to share publicly may be visible to other users, including:

  • Your username
  • Your display name
  • Your profile photo
  • Your comments
  • Photos you upload
  • Perfume-related content you share
  • Users you follow or users who follow you, depending on the app’s design

Please do not upload or post information that you do not want other users to see.

5. User-Generated Content and Moderation

Users may post photos, comments, and other content in Fragry. To help keep the community safe, we may review, remove, restrict, or report content that violates our rules, harms other users, or violates applicable laws.

Users may be able to report inappropriate content or abusive behavior. We may review reported content and take action, including removing content, limiting visibility, suspending accounts, or restricting access to Fragry.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use service providers that process information on our behalf to help us operate, secure, and improve Fragry. Depending on how you use the app, these may include:

  • Authentication providers, such as Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In, if used
  • Hosting, storage, and database providers
  • Crash reporting and diagnostics tools, including Sentry or similar tools
  • Customer support or moderation tools

These service providers are authorized to use information only as needed to provide services to us. Their practices may also be governed by their own privacy policies.

7. Advertising, Subscriptions, and Payments

Fragry is currently free.

At this time, Fragry does not show third-party ads, does not offer subscriptions, and does not offer one-time in-app purchases.

If this changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and any required App Store or Google Play privacy disclosures.

8. Device Permissions

Fragry may request access to device permissions only when needed for app features. For example, photo access may be requested when you choose to upload a perfume photo.

You can manage permissions such as Photos, Camera, Notifications, and other device permissions in your iOS or Android settings.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide Fragry, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our terms, prevent abuse, and improve the app.

Where Fragry offers in-app account deletion, using that feature starts removal of account data from our live systems. Some backups, logs, or records may persist for a limited period before automatic deletion.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at info@fragry.com. Please include the app name, your username if applicable, and the email or sign-in method tied to your account. We will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Children

Fragry is not intended for children under 13 years old, or the minimum age required in your country.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without any required parental consent. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us at info@fragry.com and we will take appropriate steps to delete the information where required by law.

11. International Users

Fragry may be used by users in different countries. Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than where you live. These countries may have different data protection laws.

Where required, we take steps to protect your information according to applicable privacy laws.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

Users should protect their accounts and avoid sharing sensitive personal information publicly.

13. Your Choices

Depending on the features available in Fragry, you may be able to:

  • Edit your profile information
  • Delete uploaded photos or comments
  • Unfollow users
  • Change account settings
  • Request account deletion
  • Contact us about privacy questions

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we may notify users through the app, website, or other appropriate means.

The updated policy will be posted with a new “Last Updated” date. Continued use of Fragry after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
